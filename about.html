About Sean Barboza

Character-driven speculative fiction built around survival, consequence, and the people who remain.

Sean Barboza is an independent author who writes character-driven speculative fiction. His stories focus on people forced to make difficult choices after the familiar world has been stripped away.

His fiction moves through pandemic collapse, post-apocalyptic horror, and a far-future American frontier, but the center of each story remains the same: ordinary people trying to decide what kind of person they will be when survival is no longer guaranteed.

Sean began writing through an interest in narrative-driven video games and the worlds behind them, eventually turning that interest into novels of his own.
