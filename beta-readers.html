Beta Readers

Interested in reading an unpublished manuscript and giving story-focused feedback? Join the interest list.

What Beta Reading Means

Beta readers read a manuscript before publication and tell me what worked, what dragged, what was confusing, and where the story lost or held their attention. You do not need to be an editor. Useful, straightforward reader reactions are what matter most.

This page is an interest list for future manuscripts. Signing up does not mean a book is currently ready for beta reading, and it does not guarantee that every volunteer will receive every manuscript.

If I have a project that looks like a good fit, I may contact you with the book description, expected turnaround time, and reading format before you decide whether to participate.
